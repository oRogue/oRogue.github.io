## Work Experience

### Game Design Internship @  Nimbus Games
### _July 2025 – October 2025_

During my internship at Nimbus Games, I contributed to the design, testing, and refinement of gameplay content for an upcoming title. My responsibilities focused on level design, encounter design, player guidance, and quality assurance for a different title.

**Player Encounter Design**
- Worked on designing and improving player encounters to create engaging gameplay scenarios that balanced challenge, pacing, and player satisfaction. Iterated on encounter designs through playtesting and feedback sessions to ensure a positive player experience.

**First Act Level Design & Game Flow**

- Planned and documented the gameplay flow for the first act of the game. Designed a clear "golden path" that guided players toward objectives using environmental cues, level layout, and natural curiosity rather than explicit instructions. This helped maintain player immersion while ensuring progression remained intuitive.

**Quality Assurance & Player Experience Analysis**

- Performed extensive QA testing on gameplay features and level content, identifying bugs, progression blockers, and usability issues. Produced detailed feedback reports and collaborated with team members to improve game quality, balance, and player experience.
